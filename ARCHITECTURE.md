# 🌌 GURUVERSE

> An Interactive AI Engineering Universe

Version: 1.0 (Locked)
Status: Active Development

---

# Vision

GURUVERSE is not a traditional portfolio.

It is an immersive engineering universe that showcases software engineering,
artificial intelligence, cybersecurity, research, and storytelling through
interactive experiences.

The goal is not simply to present projects but to let visitors explore them.

---

# Design Philosophy

Minimal Interface.

Cinematic Experience.

Every animation must communicate something.

Every interaction must have a purpose.

# Architecture Decision Records (ADR)

Every significant decision is recorded before implementation.

No implementation begins until the decision is approved.

Examples:

ADR-001: Astro + React architecture
ADR-002: Navigation design
ADR-003: Hero experience
ADR-004: GURU-BOT behavior
