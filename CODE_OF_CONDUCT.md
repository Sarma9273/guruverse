# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to fostering an open, welcoming, and respectful community.

Everyone participating in Guruverse should feel safe and valued.

---

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful.
- Be constructive.
- Help others learn.
- Welcome different perspectives.
- Communicate professionally.

---

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment
- Personal attacks
- Discrimination
- Trolling
- Spam

---

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for enforcing this Code of Conduct and may remove content or restrict participation when necessary.

---

## Contact

For questions regarding this Code of Conduct, please contact the project maintainer.

---

Thank you for helping make Guruverse a positive engineering community.
